## Evidence still needed

- Exact PMMA grade/color spectra, thickness tolerances, coatings/additives, and supplier lot identity.
- Instrument and holder drawings or measured geometry, actual beam footprint and export format.
- Actual repeatability, mounting sensitivity, spatial dependence and stability.
- Evidence supporting any assigned reference values, traceability or cross-instrument agreement.
- Independent production and interlaboratory data before reproducibility claims.
- A broader prior-art review before asserting scientific novelty; manuscript work remains outside this repository's immediate tasks.

Use the analysis/decision template to register new evidence, including source URL/version, claim scope, unresolved assumptions and experimental consequences.
