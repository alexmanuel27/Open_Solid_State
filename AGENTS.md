# Repository instructions

- Keep repository documentation, identifiers, code comments, and technical findings in English. Conversation may be in Spanish.
- This is a technical research repository; manuscript drafting is outside its current scope.
- Prioritize OSORK-ABS. Do not implement OSORK-FLUO or OSORK-TURB before sufficient ABS evidence and an explicit scope decision.
- The selected architecture is direct insertion of a PMMA piece on a printed square base, without a surrounding cuvette. Nominal 3 mm PMMA thickness and 5 mm base height are candidates, not validated dimensions.
- Do not infer holder dimensions from a nominal 10 mm cuvette optical path. Measure geometry, beam coverage, orientation, and seating before releasing fabrication files.
- Distinguish published evidence, manufacturer specifications, hypotheses, estimates, and measured results. Cite primary sources for external technical claims.
- Do not claim certification, traceability, calibration, universal compatibility, or material stability without evidence supporting the specific claim.
- Never modify original acquisition files through processing. Keep derived outputs separate and record exclusions, processing, and provenance.
- Prefer the smallest useful implementation. Do not scaffold packages, dependencies, CAD exports, or analyses before their inputs and requirements are known.
- When non-trivial analysis code is introduced, leave a small runnable check that would fail if its scientific logic breaks.
- Record deviations and failures. Do not silently change a frozen experimental protocol or acceptance criterion after viewing confirmatory results.
- Follow the file-type licensing scopes in LICENSE.md and preserve third-party notices.
